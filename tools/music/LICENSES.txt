PROJECT WINGNUT soundtrack — "Ceiling Unlimited" — third-party material and licences
=====================================================================================

Composition, orchestration and all code in tools/music/ (score.mjs, sampler, mixer, mastering, player js/music.js)
were written for PROJECT WINGNUT. The melody and harmony are original; nothing is quoted from existing music.

Instrument samples (used only at build time; their rendered result ships in assets/music/ and js/music_data.js)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VSCO 2: Community Edition (Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 CE), by Versilian Studios LLC / Sam Gossner,
with Ivy Audio / Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote.
  Source:   https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE  (WAV samples from branch "master", SFZ mappings from branch "SFZ")
  Homepage: http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html
  Licence:  Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication) — copy in downloads/vsco2ce-sfz/LICENSE.
            Readme terms: "You are permitted to use these samples for ANY purpose. We ask that you do not sell the
            samples directly ... Please provide credit to Versilian Studios/Sam Gossner, and/or Ivy Audio/Simon
            Dalzell where applicable, and link to the VSCO: CE homepage."  -> credited here and in js/music_data.js.
  Used:     violin/viola/cello sections (sustain, spiccato, tremolo, pizzicato), solo contrabass, harp, F horn,
            trumpet, tenor trombone, tuba (sustain + staccato), flute, timpani (hits + rolls), concert bass drum,
            suspended / crash cymbals, gong, snare, large ethnic drum ("taiko" role), tenor drums.
  Downloaded to tools/music/downloads/ (treated as data: parsed by our own WAV/SFZ readers, nothing executed).

Derived "virtual" horn samples (tools/music/out/virtual/) are formant-preserving re-pitches of the CC0 horn
recordings, generated by lib/formant.mjs to fill the library's C4..D5 gap.

No other third-party audio, soundfonts or npm packages are used. ffmpeg (system tool, LGPL/GPL build) is only
invoked at build time to encode Ogg Opus / MP3; it is not shipped. The choir pad is synthesised (lib/choir.mjs).
